## Official Resources

_Official Kyoto Animation websites, catalogs, stores, publishing resources, channels, and project pages._

- [KyoAni Shop!](https://kyoanishop.com/) - Kyoto Animation's official online store for home video, books, art and production-related goods, merchandise, and KA Esuma Bunko titles.
- [KyoaniChannel](https://www.youtube.com/@KyoaniChannel) - Kyoto Animation's official YouTube channel for trailers, commercials, project announcements, music videos, making-of features, and other studio videos.
- [Kyoto Animation Corporate Profile](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/en/company/profile/) - Official company facts covering the studio's founding, business activities, headquarters, and corporate leadership.
- [Kyoto Animation Information Archive](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/information/) - The studio's official archive of announcements, shop updates, and event news, with category and year-based browsing.
- [Kyoto Animation Official Website](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/) - The studio's main first-party hub for news, company information, recruitment, events, works, staff content, publishing, and official shop links.
- [Kyoto Animation Professional Training School](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/school/) - The studio-run training program for animation and background art, with courses taught by active Kyoto Animation creators and information on curriculum and applications.
- [Kyoto Animation Recruitment](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/recruit/) - The studio's official careers portal with workplace information, role guides, current recruitment categories, application requirements, and employment details.
- [Kyoto Animation Works](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/en/works/) - The official filmography of Kyoto Animation productions, with release information and title pages covering staff, cast, and official links.

## Production and Animation

_Resources about Kyoto Animation's production process, animation craft, direction, cinematography, color, backgrounds, compositing, and related techniques._

- [Kyoto Animation Business Overview](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/company/business/) - The studio's official overview of its in-house animation pipeline, merchandise development, publishing, and professional training operations.
- [Kyoto Animation CM Library](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/company/cm/) - An official archive of Kyoto Animation's corporate animated commercials, with broadcast dates and individual video entries.
- [Kyoto Animation Job Profiles](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/recruit/job/) - Official descriptions of animation, digital paint, background, photography and 2DCG, 3DCG, management, publishing, web, and other studio roles.
- [Kyoto Animation Professional Training School Blog](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/school/blog/) - A Japanese-language official blog with first-hand posts from trainees and instructors about animation study, background art, assignments, and preparation for professional work.
